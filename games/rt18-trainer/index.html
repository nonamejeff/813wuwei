Shift Knob

The face below matches the truck labels.

Same H Pattern Twice

Low range uses the H pattern once. High range uses the same stick holes again for 5 through 8.

Splitter Stays in the Same Hole

The splitter is the easy move. Your hand stays in the same stick hole and the truck changes to the higher or lower gear in that hole.

Listen for the Engine

Use the RPM readout like engine sound. Too low means it is lugging. Too high means it wants the next gear.
